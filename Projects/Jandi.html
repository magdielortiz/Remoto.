Jandi

Jandi is an online marketplace that connects customers with local professionals for a wide range of services, such as home improvement, cleaning, tutoring, home healthcare, and personal wellness. The brand needed to convey clarity, trust, and approachability, reflecting its goal of simplifying how people find and connect with reliable local experts in Puerto Rico.

The online marketplace name, visual identity and design system extends seamlessly into the web app, and across all printed matter and brand collaterals, emphasizing intuitive navigation, clean typography, and a friendly yet professional tone that supports both customers and service providers.

Partner: Luis A. Díaz-Alejandro & Magdiel Ortiz Soto
