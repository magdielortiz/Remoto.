Taboas

Taboas Tax & Business Advisors is a boutique business practice that provides advice, guidance, and support to clients to help them comply with the fiscal responsibilities of doing business within Puerto Rico’s commercial landscape.

As the firm expanded its services and client base, a new brand identity was required to better reflect its expertise in cryptocurrency, blockchain, export services, creative industries, real estate, construction, investing, and trading. To meet this need, a symbol was developed to convey security and sophistication, supported by a refined typographic system and a modern visual language designed to resonate with a global corporate audience.

Partner: Luis A. Díaz-Alejandro & Magdiel Ortiz Soto
Services: Visual Identity, Design System, Web Design
